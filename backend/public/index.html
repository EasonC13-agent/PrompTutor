Features

Everything you need for smarter AI use

PrompTutor combines privacy-preserving research tools with real-time guidance that helps students develop better prompting habits.

🧠

Real-Time Prompt Guidance

Detects answer-seeking prompts (e.g., "solve this for me") and suggests rephrased, help-seeking alternatives before you send.

🔒

Privacy-First by Design

Your email is SHA-256 hashed client-side and never sent to our servers. All research data is keyed to an irreversible anonymous ID.

✅

Granular Consent

Toggle data sharing on or off per conversation. Data collection is strictly opt-in — nothing is sent without your explicit action.

📊

Transparent Data Access

View all data you've contributed at any time from the popup. Delete everything with one click — permanently and immediately.

🌐

Multi-Platform Support

Works across 10+ AI chat platforms including ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and more — no extra setup required for each.

🎓

Research-Grade Logging

Contributes anonymized conversation data to CMU research on student–AI interaction patterns and learning outcomes.

How It Works

Up and running in under a minute

No configuration required. Just install, sign in, and start using AI as you normally would.

1

Install the Extension

Add PrompTutor to Chrome from the Web Store. It activates automatically on supported AI platforms.

2

Sign In with Google

Authenticate via Google OAuth. Your email is anonymized on-device before any data is associated with you.

3

Choose Your Mode

Select Data Collection Only to passively contribute research data, or add Prompt Guidance for real-time feedback.

4

Chat & Learn

Use AI as usual. When guidance is on, the extension will flag answer-seeking prompts and suggest better alternatives.

Privacy

Built with your privacy in mind

PrompTutor follows the DELICATE ethical framework for learning analytics. Your data is yours — always.

🔑

Client-Side Anonymization

SHA-256 hashing happens in your browser. Your real email never leaves your device.

🎛️

You Stay in Control

Toggle sharing on or off at any time from the popup or the floating indicator on any AI page.

🗑️

Right to Deletion

Delete all your data from our servers instantly with one click in the extension popup.

📡

No Tracking Outside AI Sites

The extension only activates on the listed AI platforms — it reads nothing from any other website.

🏛️

IRB-Approved Research

This study is conducted under oversight of the Carnegie Mellon University Institutional Review Board.

📄

Full Policy Transparency

Advancing the Science of AI-Assisted Learning

PrompTutor is a research tool designed at Carnegie Mellon University to study how students interact with AI chatbots in educational contexts.

Aggregate data contributed through the extension helps researchers understand prompting patterns and develop better scaffolding strategies for AI literacy.

PrompTutor: A Browser Extension for Data Collection and Real-Time Intervention in Student-Chatbot Interactions. L@S 2026 Work-in-Progress.
